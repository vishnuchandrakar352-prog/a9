The Five Classic Silhouettes

A silhouette is the outline a garment makes, seen from across the room. Learn the five classics and you can read almost any outfit at a glance.

01

A-line

Fitted at the shoulders or waist and widening gently toward the hem, like the capital letter A. It skims the hips, suits almost every figure and moves well. A-line skirts and dresses are a reliable starting point for any wardrobe.

02

Column and sheath

A column runs straight from shoulder to hem.

  • Creates one long, uninterrupted line.
  • A sheath is a column that follows the body more closely.
  • Works beautifully in a single colour from head to toe.

03

Hourglass and fit-and-flare

Both mark the waist. The hourglass balances shoulders and hips around a defined middle; fit-and-flare fits through the bodice and then flares from the waist, often with a full skirt. These shapes add curve and movement.

04

Empire

An empire line sets the waist just under the bust, with fabric falling freely below. It lengthens the legs and is comfortable to wear. Look for soft fabrics that drape rather than stand away from the body.

05

Oversized and cocoon

Volume is placed on purpose: a dropped shoulder, a wide body, a rounded cocoon coat. Balance it with something slimmer elsewhere, such as fitted trousers under a big coat, so the shape looks deliberate.
